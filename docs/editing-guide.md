# Editing your website

A short guide to changing the text and images on araksan.com yourself.

## Signing in

1. Go to **araksan.com/admin**.
2. Enter your password. If two-step sign-in is on, also enter the six-digit code from your
   authenticator app.
3. You stay signed in for 12 hours. Use **Sign out** at the top right on a shared computer.

After five wrong attempts, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes.

## Making a change

1. Choose a page on the left: Home, About, Consulting and so on.
2. Each piece of text has two boxes side by side: **English** and **Français**. A box marked
   *empty* has no text yet.
3. Change what you need, then press **Save changes** at the bottom of the screen.
4. The website updates straight away. Use **Open page ↗** to check it.

Changed your mind before saving? Press **Discard**. Every save also keeps a copy of the
previous version, so your developer can restore earlier text if needed.

## Formatting text

- **New paragraph:** leave a blank line between paragraphs.
- **A line set apart:** start a paragraph with `> ` (a "greater than" sign and a space).
  On About this becomes an elegant pull line; in the privacy notice it becomes a heading.
- **Italics:** wrap words in asterisks, for example `Perspective changes *everything.*`

## Images

1. Find the image and press **Replace image**.
2. Choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP file up to 15 MB. Large photos are resized automatically.
3. Fill in the **image description** in both languages. It is read aloud to people who use
   screen readers and helps search engines. Describe what the picture shows.
4. Press **Save changes**.

Use only images you have the right to publish.

## Projects (Productions page)

Under **Productions → Projects** you can edit each project, change its order with ↑ and ↓,
add one with **+ Add**, or remove one. Keep descriptions to what you are happy to make
public.

## Settings

- **Show the Perspectives page:** adds Perspectives to the menu. Only published essays appear.
- **Show the email address on the site:** turn on once contact@araksan.com is working.
- **Show the quote on the homepage:** the sentence above the footer on the homepage.

## Essays (Perspectives)

1. Go to **Perspectives → Essays** and press **+ Add**.
2. Fill in the title, summary and text in both languages, and a date.
3. **Web address:** short lowercase words joined by hyphens, for example
   `what-a-market-report-cannot-tell-you`. This becomes araksan.com/en/perspectives/…
4. Tick **Published** when it is ready, then save. Make sure **Show the Perspectives page**
   is on under Settings.

## Search & sharing

The title and description for each page, as they appear in Google and when a link is shared
on LinkedIn, WhatsApp or email. Keep descriptions to about 150 characters.
